Privacy & disclaimer

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · På svenska ↓

Not an official Pokémon product

cardtrainer.io is an unofficial, free fan project made by one person. It is not affiliated with, endorsed, sponsored or approved by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Creatures Inc. or GAME FREAK. Pokémon, the Pokémon card game, card names and card images are trademarks and © of their respective owners.

Card data and images are loaded from the Pokémon TCG API. Prices are rough estimates taken from that API (Cardmarket / TCGplayer data) and can be wrong or out of date – don't use them to buy or sell.

The short version

  • You can play as a guest. Then everything stays on your device.
  • If you sign in with Google, a copy of your progress is kept in the cloud so it follows you between devices.
  • The leaderboard only shows your nickname (or "Guest") and your score – never your name or email.
  • No ads, no selling of data, no tracking cookies.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account.

What is stored, and where

On your device (always): your progress (cards guessed right, history, repeats), saved cards, daily card results, best streak and settings, plus downloaded card lists so the game loads fast. This stays on your device until you reset progress, clear your browser data or remove the app.

In the cloud (only if you sign in): when you sign in with Google, our database provider Supabase receives your email address, name and profile picture link from Google to create your login. We also store a copy of the device data listed above and your leaderboard nickname, if you choose one. This is kept until you delete your account.

Who else is involved

Loading the game means your device talks directly to a few services. Like any website, they see your IP address and basic browser information when it connects:

  • GitHub Pages – hosts the website.
  • Pokémon TCG API (pokemontcg.io) – card lists, card images and prices.
  • Supabase – logins and the cloud copy of your progress (only if you sign in).
  • Google – the "Sign in with Google" step (only if you sign in).
  • GoatCounter – simple visitor statistics (how many people visit and which game modes are used). It uses no cookies and is built not to collect personal data.

Voice answers (off unless you turn it on): uses your browser's or phone's own speech recognition. Depending on your device, that may send the audio to Apple or Google to turn it into text. cardtrainer.io only receives the recognised words and stores no recordings.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything in the cloud: Settings → Account → Delete account. Your login, cloud progress and nickname are removed straight away.
  • Delete what's on your device: Settings → Reset progress, or clear the site's data / remove the app.
  • Change your nickname any time in Settings → Account.
  • You can ask what data is stored about you, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted, via the contact below. If you think your data is handled wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Children

Anyone can play as a guest. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian before signing in with an account.

Kort sagt

  • Du kan spela som gäst – då stannar allt på din enhet.
  • Loggar du in med Google sparas en kopia av dina framsteg i molnet så att de följer med mellan enheter. Supabase (vår databastjänst) får då din e-postadress, ditt namn och en länk till din profilbild från Google.

  • Röstsvar (av som standard) använder webbläsarens eller telefonens taligenkänning, som beroende på enhet kan skicka ljudet till Apple eller Google. cardtrainer.io får bara de igenkända orden och sparar inga inspelningar.
  • Radera kontot och molndatan när som helst: Inställningar → Konto → Radera konto. Data på enheten tar du bort med Nollställ framsteg eller genom att rensa webbplatsdata.
